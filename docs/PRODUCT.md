# Product

Goosegrass is a native macOS customer appointment workspace for individual operators who need to retain customer leads, appointment history, reminders, and follow-up without depending on a server.

Its core promise is to prevent missed customers, forgotten appointments, overwritten history, and unrecoverable local data. Customer is the durable business entity; appointments, activities, and follow-ups form that customer's history.

## V1 boundaries

V1 is local first, offline first, and single user. It does not include login, cloud sync, teams, mobile apps, automated messaging, payments, platform scraping, or a web backend.

## Success loop

```text
Capture customer -> detect possible duplicate -> contact -> create appointment
-> remind -> arrive/reschedule/no-show/cancel -> follow up -> rebook
```
